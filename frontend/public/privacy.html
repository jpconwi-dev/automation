Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What this app is

"automation" is a private tool. Its owner uses it to make short videos and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel and Facebook Page. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What access it asks for

The app asks the owner for one Google permission, to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read your YouTube account, your email, your contacts or any other Google data.

What data is collected or stored

The app does not collect, sell, share or store personal data about visitors. The only credentials it keeps are the owner's own sign-in tokens, which are stored as private secrets in the owner's own GitHub account and are used only to upload the owner's own videos.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
