Put your own royalty-free music files here (.mp3, .wav, .m4a, .ogg, .flac).

How it works
- If this folder has audio files, each video uses ONE of them, picked at random, instead of the built-in music.
- The track loops if the video is longer than the song, fades in and out, and is lowered under the voice automatically.
- If the folder is empty, the built-in music is used (choose its style with MUSIC_STYLE).

Use only music you have the right to use on Facebook.
- Do NOT use popular commercial songs or "trending sounds" you downloaded. Facebook can mute or block the video,
  limit its reach, or strike your Page.
- Safer sources: Pixabay Music and the YouTube Audio Library. Read the licence on each track
  (some require credit, and some do not allow monetised use).
- Keep files under about 10 MB each so the repo stays small.
